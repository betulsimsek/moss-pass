Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Moss Pass does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data.

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • The game works fully offline and makes no network requests.
  • Your best wave and chosen difficulty are saved only on your device and are removed when you delete the app.

Children: the game collects no data from anyone, including children.

Questions: github.com/betulsimsek/moss-pass/issues. If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
